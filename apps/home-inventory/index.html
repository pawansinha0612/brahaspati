Is your contents cover enough?

Compare the total of your list with the contents sum insured on your policy. This list uses what you paid; replacing things new today may cost more, and most homes have far more small items than anyone lists.

Claim pack

Tick the items lost or damaged, then print or save a list with photos, serial numbers and values to send to your insurer.

Missing proof

Insurers usually ask for a photo and a receipt or bank statement; serial numbers help police and insurers for stolen electronics and tools.

Warranties ending soon

The calendar file has one all-day event on each warranty end date, with an alert 30 days before. In Australia you also have rights under the Australian Consumer Law after a warranty ends if a product fails sooner than it reasonably should.
