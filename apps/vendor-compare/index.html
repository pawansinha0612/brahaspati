How to compare costs

Every price is converted to the same GST basis (10%) before adding up. Total = one-off costs + monthly and annual fees for every year + per-unit rate × your volume, with any yearly price rise compounded. If a quote doesn’t say whether GST is included we treat it as excluding GST and flag it.

Criteria and weights

Weights don’t need to add up to 100 — each one counts in proportion. Tick “from cost” to score a criterion automatically from total cost (cheapest = 5, dearest = 1).
