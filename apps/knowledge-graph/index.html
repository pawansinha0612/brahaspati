Backup

A backup holds your documents’ text and what was found in them, so you can move to another browser.

Your documents are read and stored in this browser only (IndexedDB) — nothing is uploaded. Names, organisations and projects are found with fixed patterns (capitalised names, “Pty Ltd”, “Project …”), so it will miss some and occasionally pick up a heading as a name. Scanned PDFs without a text layer can’t be read. If you turn on the optional AI helper, the ✨ button sends only the document you pick to the AI you chose.
