Decision Simulator

Compare two to five options for a business decision. Enter what each option costs and returns as a low, likely and high guess, add the risks, and see the likely cash outcome, the chance each option comes out best, and which number matters most.

Your decisions are saved in this browser only. Use Export to keep a copy or move it to another device.

Discount rate: what your money could earn elsewhere or costs you to borrow (6–12% is common for small businesses). It makes later dollars count a little less than dollars today. The seed makes the simulation repeatable — change it to check the answer is stable.

Up to 5 options.

Things money doesn't capture

List the factors that matter (customer experience, stress, flexibility…) and how much each matters (weight 0–5). Then score each option 0–10 on each. The result is a weighted score out of 100, shown next to the money result — it does not change the money numbers.
