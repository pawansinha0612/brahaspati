Smart Trip Planner

Tell it who’s going, your budget and pace, add the things you’d like to do with their opening hours and prices — and it builds a realistic day-by-day plan with travel time, lunch, kids’ naps and rest breaks.

Your plans stay in this browser. This app doesn’t look up live opening hours, prices or weather — you enter them, and starter ideas are generic examples to edit.

Not scheduled

How it works: must-dos go first, then the rest. Each activity goes to a day already in the same area if it fits (less travel), otherwise the emptiest day. It keeps to opening hours, your day times, lunch, naps and pace, adds travel time between areas, a 20-minute rest after long outings when kids are under 10, and skips outdoor things on days you mark as rainy. Long outings (2.5 h+) include lunch, and 4 h+ include the nap.
