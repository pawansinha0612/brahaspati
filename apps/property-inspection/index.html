Property Inspection Assistant

Walk through a rental room by room on your phone: rate each item clean, undamaged and working, add photos and notes, get the tenant to sign on screen, then print the condition report. Compare entry with exit, turn problems into a maintenance list and keep follow-ups on track.

How it works and its limits
  • Each property lists its rooms. A new inspection builds a checklist from those rooms (kitchens get an oven and rangehood, bathrooms get taps and an exhaust fan, and so on). You can add or remove items and rooms.
  • "Raise maintenance for problems" creates a maintenance item for every item marked N, with a suggested trade, priority and due date (urgent: 1 day, high: 7, medium: 14, low: 30). Check the suggestions — they are simple keyword rules.
  • The entry vs exit comparison flags items that were fine at entry and marked N at exit. It cannot judge fair wear and tear.
  • This is not your state's official condition report form. Use it as your working record, or copy the details into the official form where one is required.
  • Photos are shrunk and stored in this browser only. Large inspections with many photos can fill storage — download backups and remove old photos you no longer need.
