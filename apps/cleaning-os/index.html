Cleaning Business OS

Set up each site with its own checklist, book weekly or fortnightly cleans, let the roster auto-allocate cleaners fairly, tick off jobs on a phone with photos and the client's signature, catch missed and late jobs, and total each cleaner's hours for payroll.

How it works and its limits
  • Recurring cleans: open a job and choose Repeat… (weekly, fortnightly or monthly). Copies keep the time, cleaner and a fresh checklist.
  • Auto-allocate only fills jobs with no cleaner. It respects each cleaner's days, hours and weekly maximum, keeps 30 minutes between jobs, never double-books, and spreads hours evenly. Anything it cannot place is listed with the reason.
  • A job is "late" when the arrival time is more than 15 minutes after the booked start. Jobs still "Scheduled" after their day show as not marked done.
  • Everything lives in this browser — cleaners cannot see the roster on their own phones unless you print or send it. There is no login, GPS check-in or payroll lodgement.
  • Pay figures are hours × rate only. Award rates, penalties, super and tax are up to you or your payroll software.
