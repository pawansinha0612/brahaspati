Contract Risk Scanner

Upload or paste a contract to find the clauses that usually cost small businesses money: automatic renewals, notice periods, payment terms, price rises, unlimited liability, indemnities, restraints and personal guarantees. Get the key dates for your calendar and a list of questions to ask.

Not legal advice. This tool reads the words with fixed rules — it does not understand the contract like a lawyer does. It can miss clauses, misread them, or flag things that are fine. Use it to prepare questions, then get a lawyer to review anything important before you sign.

The contract is read inside this browser and never uploaded (unless you press the optional AI button with a Gemini key turned on). Saved scans stay in this browser only.

Drop a contract here or click to choose — PDF, Word (.docx) or text file.
