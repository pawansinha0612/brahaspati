Business Process Mapper

Describe how a job gets done — type it, paste meeting notes, dictate it, or upload an event log — and get a swimlane process diagram, a ready-to-use SOP and ideas to make the process faster.

Everything stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded. The diagram drawing library (Mermaid) is downloaded once from a public CDN.

How to write steps

  • Who does it: start with the role — Admin: checks stock, or end with by the warehouse. Roles become swimlanes.
  • Decisions: If out of stock, notify customer, else create invoice. Use go back to step 2 for loops and (end) where it stops.
  • Time: add (10 min), (2 hours) or (1 day).
  • Systems: mention the tool — Xero, Excel, email, Shopify — and it is listed in the SOP.
  • Words like wait, approve and copy into are flagged as waits, approvals and re-keying.
