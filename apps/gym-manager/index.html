Gym & Studio Manager

Members, memberships and holds, a weekly class timetable with capacity and an automatic waitlist, a front-desk check-in, trainer hours, renewal reminders, and a retention view that shows who hasn't been in lately.

How it works and its limits
  • Fixed terms end after the minimum term (annual = 12 months, class packs after their valid days). Every hold day pushes the end date and the next payment date back by a day; overlapping holds are counted once.
  • Ongoing plans (weekly, fortnightly, monthly with no lock-in) have no end date — the Renewals tab lists the next payment dates instead, and you can add them to your calendar as an .ics file.
  • Booking a full class puts the member on the waitlist. When someone cancels, the first person waiting is moved in automatically — but this app cannot text or email them, so let them know.
  • "At risk" = an active membership and no check-in or attended class for 14 days or more (members on hold are left out).
  • Health information: only record that a pre-exercise screening was received and its date. Keep the signed form itself somewhere secure — do not type medical details in here.
  • Everything lives in this browser: no member app or online booking, no card payments or direct debits, no sync between devices. Reminders need this page open, or download the .ics file into your calendar.
