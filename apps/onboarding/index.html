Employee Onboarding

A checklist for every new starter: what to do before day 1, on day 1, in week 1 and over the first three months — who does it and by when. Track paperwork received, training and expiry dates, and equipment handed out, then print a welcome pack.

Everything stays in this browser. Never type tax file numbers, bank details or ID numbers here — this tool only records whether a form was received. Keep those in your payroll system.
