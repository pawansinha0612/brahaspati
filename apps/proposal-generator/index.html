Proposal Generator

Paste a client's email, brief or request for quote. The tool picks out what they need, their deadlines and budget, then drafts a proposal you can edit: scope, deliverables, a week-by-week timeline, assumptions, risks and three ways to price it.

Briefs, proposals and your business details are saved in this browser only. Use Export to keep a copy.

Reading is rule-based: it looks for words such as need, must, should and want, bullet points, dollar amounts and dates. Always check what it found.
