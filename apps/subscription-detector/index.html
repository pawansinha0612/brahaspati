Subscription Leak Detector

Import a bank statement and see every repeating charge — streaming, apps, gyms, phone plans — with what each one costs you a year, which ones went up in price, and a simple plan to cancel the ones you don’t use.

Your statement is read inside this browser and never uploaded. Uses the same statement reader as the Money Command Centre.

A bank statement can’t show whether you still use something. For each charge, decide: Keep, Cancel or Review (not sure — remind me before the next charge).

Found from charges to the same business on a regular cycle (weekly to yearly). Needs about three months of history; yearly charges need more than a year. Rent and transfers are left out.
