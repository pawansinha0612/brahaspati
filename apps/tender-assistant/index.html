Tender Response Assistant

Drop in the request for tender (or quote) and get every “must” and “should” listed, the evaluation criteria and weightings, the closing date and the submission rules. Then track compliance, plan your pages, reuse your best past answers and decide whether it’s worth bidding at all.

Tender documents are read on your device and never uploaded. Extraction is rule-based — it finds sentences with words like must, shall and should — so always read the full tender too.
