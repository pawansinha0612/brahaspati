Pet Business Manager

For groomers, dog walkers and pet sitters. Keep every pet's care notes, allergies and vaccinations in one place, price by size, book weekly walks, catch double bookings and overdue vaccinations, send a "pupdate" after each visit, and track walk passes and invoices.

How it works and its limits
  • Pet size comes from weight: small under 10 kg, medium 10–25 kg, large 25–45 kg, giant 45 kg and over. Choosing a pet and a service fills the price, length and visit checklist.
  • Vaccination reminders appear 30 days before the next due date. If you leave "Next due" empty, it uses a usual interval (C3 every 3 years, other vaccines yearly) — always prefer the date on the vet certificate. Bookings for pets whose vaccination will be overdue on the day are flagged.
  • A staff member cannot be booked for two things at once, unless both bookings are ticked as a group walk.
  • Weekly walks: open a booking and choose Repeat…. Packages count every booking linked to them except cancelled ones (no-shows use a visit).
  • Groom reminders use the last finished groom plus each pet's groom interval (6–8 weeks is typical).
  • Prices are entered including GST; invoices show GST as 1/11 of the total when you are registered. This is not accounting software.
  • Everything lives in this browser: no login, no sync between phones, no online booking for customers and no automatic texts. Reminders need this page open, or download the .ics file into your calendar. Share a pupdate by copying its text or downloading it as HTML.
