Email Assistant

Paste a pile of emails to see what needs you first, draft a polite reply from a template in the tone you want, and check your own emails before you hit send.

This isn’t connected to any mailbox. You copy emails in and copy replies out — nothing is sent, read or stored anywhere else.

Start each one with its “From:” line (as when you copy from Outlook or Gmail), or separate them with a line of ---.
