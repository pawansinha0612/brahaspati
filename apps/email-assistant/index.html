Inbox Intelligence

Paste a pile of emails to see what needs you first, the promises you (and others) made, questions nobody answered and who you’re waiting on — then set dated follow-ups, draft a polite reply and check your tone before you hit send.

This isn’t connected to any mailbox. You copy emails in and copy replies out — nothing is sent or uploaded. Follow-ups you keep are saved in this browser only.

Start each one with its “From:” line (as when you copy from Outlook or Gmail), or separate them with a line of ---.

Found in the emails you last sorted. Promises are sentences like “I’ll send…”, “let me check…”, “we will get back to you by…”. A question counts as answered when a later reply in the same conversation talks about the same thing.

Conversations

Every promise and unanswered question becomes a follow-up with a date. Dates come from the email (“by Thursday” in an email sent Monday = that Thursday); with no date, it’s 1–3 business days after the email. Follow-ups stay here (in this browser) until you tick them off or delete them.

Everything happens in your browser. Sorting, promises, questions and tone checks use fixed word lists and patterns, not AI, so they can miss sarcasm, context or a promise worded unusually. If you turn on the optional AI helper, the ✨ buttons send only the text you’re rewriting to the AI you chose.
